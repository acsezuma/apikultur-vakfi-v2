There are different paths to the future.

Apiculture Future is the Apiculture Foundation’s Education, Science and Future Support Programme, created to support students, young researchers and high-quality work that will contribute to the future of bees, beekeeping, pollination, biodiversity, agriculture, food, the environment and rural development.

It offers different paths of support for different needs, from various stages of education to scientific research, and from international development to fieldwork.

Which support is right for you?

Answer three short questions and we will show you the support paths that may suit you. This is not an application; it simply points you to the right programme.

Programmes that may suit you

    The field you selected is within the scope of Apiculture Future programmes.

    Open calls for these programmes are published in the Open Programmes section.

    Open programmes

    Apiculture Future support is offered through periodic programmes. For each programme, eligibility, scope of support, application dates, evaluation criteria and the results process are set out in the programme announcement.

    New programmes are being prepared.

    There is currently no Apiculture Future programme open for applications. New scholarship and support programmes are announced on this page when they open.

    Pre-registration

    You can fill in the short pre-registration form so that we can contact you when programmes open.

    Pre-registration is not an application. Applications are accepted under the conditions set out in each programme announcement.

    When you press Send, your details open as a ready-made email in your email app; all you need to do is send it.

    Your email draft is ready. Once you send it, your pre-registration will reach us.

    How to apply

    Applications are free of charge. Evaluation criteria are set before a programme opens. Depending on the programme, evaluation files may be separated from identity information and more than one evaluator may be used.

    1. 01

      Find your programme

      Explore the open programmes and choose the one that suits you.

    2. 02

      Review the conditions

      Review the eligibility requirements, timeline, scope of support and evaluation criteria.

    3. 03

      Complete your application

      Complete your application online. Applying is free of charge.

    4. 04

      Evaluation

      Your application is evaluated against criteria set in advance for the relevant programme.

    5. 05

      Allocation

      Support is allocated through the Foundation’s authorised decision-making process, based on evaluation results, the programme budget and available places. Results are communicated through the channel stated in the programme announcement.

    6. 06

      Support and follow-up

      Successful candidates join the programme after the necessary verification steps; progress is followed according to the nature of the programme.

    What do we ask for in an application?

    We aim to ask applicants only for the information a programme needs. Depending on the programme, educational details, academic or professional information, motivation, financial circumstances and research or project details may be requested.

    Not every document needs to be uploaded at the first stage. Information needed only at later stages, such as identity verification and bank details, is requested when it becomes necessary.

    How do we evaluate?

    Criteria set in advance

    The evaluation criteria for each programme are announced before applications open.

    Impartial evaluation

    Evaluators are bound by rules on ethics, confidentiality and conflicts of interest.

    Anonymisation where needed

    Identity information that is not needed for evaluation is separated from evaluators wherever possible.

    Institutional decision

    Evaluators’ opinions and the Committee’s recommendations are not final allocation decisions; allocation is made through the Foundation’s authorised decision-making process.

    Our principles of trust

    Applying is free of charge.

    No applicant is charged for evaluation or allocation.

    Personal data is processed proportionately.

    Applications ask only for information needed for the process.

    Donors and evaluation are kept separate.

    Providing funding does not give the right to select applicants.

    Human decision-making is preserved.

    Final support allocation is never left solely to an automated system or artificial intelligence.

    The connection does not end when the support does.

    Apiculture Future is not only about providing financial support. Depending on the programme, beneficiaries’ education, scientific work or development is followed over time. We assess our results not only by the resources distributed, but by the outcomes in education, science, research and the field.

    Future Network

    Supported students, researchers and other beneficiaries may choose to join the Future Network, a voluntary community built on knowledge sharing, mentoring, scientific cooperation and international connections.

    Joining the Future Network is voluntary and is not a condition of any scholarship or support.

    Stand with a future, not just a student.

    Apiculture Future is a meeting point not only for those seeking support, but also for people and institutions who want to invest in the future. Individuals and institutions can contribute to education, science, research or specific thematic areas.

    Future Fund

    Donors may choose the area they support; beneficiaries are selected within the Foundation’s independent evaluation and allocation system.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who can apply?add

    The target group varies by programme. Separate calls may be opened for students, postgraduate students, young researchers, beekeepers, farmers or other candidates within the scope of specific programmes. Exact requirements are stated in each programme announcement.

    Is there an application fee?add

    No. Applying to Apiculture Future programmes is free of charge. No fee is charged for application or evaluation.

    Can I apply if I already receive another scholarship or grant?add

    This depends on the conditions of the relevant programme. Receiving a scholarship or grant from another institution does not, on its own, prevent you from applying to Apiculture Future programmes unless the programme announcement explicitly states otherwise.

    How are applications evaluated?add

    Applications are evaluated against criteria set in advance for the relevant programme. Depending on the programme, a preliminary review, assessment by independent evaluators and an institutional decision stage may apply.

    Do scholarships or grants have to be repaid?add

    Unless the programme announcement explicitly states otherwise, scholarships and grants under Apiculture Future do not have to be repaid. For purpose-specific research or project grants, recipients may be asked to use the funds for their intended purpose and to report on them.

    Can evaluators see my personal information?add

    Wherever the nature of the programme allows, identity and contact details not needed for evaluation are kept separate from evaluators.

    What is Apiculture Future?add

    Apiculture Future is the Apiculture Foundation’s Education, Science and Future Support Programme, created to support students, young researchers and high-quality education, science, research and development work.

    Are programmes always open?add

    No. Scholarship and support programmes open periodically. Programmes open for applications are published on the Apiculture Future page.

    What scholarships and grants are offered?add

    Depending on the period, programmes may include student scholarships, young researcher grants, thesis and research grants, support for scientific meetings and mobility, and programmes for education and field development.

    What documents are required?add

    Required documents vary by programme. We aim to request only the information and documents needed for evaluation at the application stage. Identity verification, bank details or additional documents may be requested at later stages.

    Can I see the evaluation criteria in advance?add

    Yes. The main evaluation criteria for a programme, and their weighting where applicable, are set out in the programme announcement.

    Can I change my information after applying?add

    During the application period, changes can be made to the fields the system allows. Options for corrections or completing documents after the application closes are set by the rules of the relevant programme.

    Does applying entitle me to support?add

    No. Applying, or exceeding the minimum evaluation score, does not on its own create a right to support. Support is allocated on the basis of evaluation results, the programme budget, available places and the Foundation’s authorised decision-making process.

    How will I learn the results?add

    Results are communicated to applicants through the channel stated in the relevant programme announcement.

    If I do not receive support, can I find out why?add

    Depending on the programme, your application status and, where appropriate, evaluation information may be shared. If you believe there has been a material error, such as in a calculation, the system or document processing, you can use the material error review mechanism set out in the programme announcement.

    What does being on the reserve list mean?add

    Reserve status means your application was found eligible for support but was not placed on the main list at the current allocation stage. Being on the reserve list does not create a definite right to support.

    Do I have to work for the Foundation in return for a scholarship?add

    No. No obligation to work, provide services, make donations or buy goods or services from the Foundation is imposed in return for a scholarship or grant.

    If I receive a research grant, do the intellectual property rights to my work pass to the Foundation?add

    Receiving support alone does not mean that the intellectual property or scientific authorship of the research automatically passes to the Apiculture Foundation. If a specific project requires a different arrangement, the conditions are stated separately.

    Are applications evaluated by artificial intelligence?add

    Digital tools may be used to organise the application process and for certain checks; final support allocation is never left solely to an automated system or artificial intelligence.

    Can donors choose scholarship holders or the researchers to be supported?add

    Donors can contribute to supporting specific areas of education, science or research. The evaluation of beneficiaries and the allocation of support are carried out within the Foundation’s institutional processes.

    What happens after I receive support?add

    Depending on the programme, education, research or development may be followed at set intervals. The aim is not only to transfer resources, but also to follow the results the support creates.

    What is the Apiculture Future Network?add

    A voluntary network that aims to bring together students, researchers, academics, beekeepers and field experts for knowledge sharing, mentoring and cooperation.

    Is joining the Future Network compulsory?add

    No. Participation is entirely voluntary. Not joining cannot be held against you in current or future scholarship and support evaluations.

    What should I do if no programme is open right now?add

    New programmes are published in the Open Programmes section of this page when they open. In the meantime, you can fill in the pre-registration form.

    We believe that investing in knowledge, science and the people who carry it forward can change the future.
